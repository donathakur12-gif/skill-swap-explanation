Why skill exchange?

Value isn't only
measured in money.

A skill exchange can make learning more social and practical. It encourages people to share experience, learn through doing and recognise value in what they already know.

  • ✓ Make learning more collaborative
  • ✓ Build confidence by teaching others
  • ✓ Practise skills in a real conversation
  • ✓ Create connections beyond your usual circle
