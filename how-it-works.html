Small steps, shared progress

Four steps.
Two people growing.

Here's how a skill exchange could work in a future SkillSwap community.

01
START WITH YOURSELF

Share what you know.

List the skills you can teach and the ones you're excited to learn. You don't need to be an expert — just be clear about what you can offer and what you'd like to practise.

Skills I can teachSkills I want to learn
02
FIND COMMON GROUND

Look for a good match.

Imagine discovering someone who can teach you video editing and wants to learn basic coding from you. A good match starts with shared goals, compatible availability and mutual interest.

Complementary skillsShared goals
03
MAKE A PLAN

Agree on the exchange.

Discuss what each person hopes to achieve, how you'll meet, how much time you can offer and what a successful session looks like. Good communication makes the exchange fair.

Set expectationsChoose a format
04
LEARN AND GIVE BACK

Exchange. Practise. Grow.

Take turns teaching and learning, share useful resources, ask questions and give kind feedback. Over time, small exchanges can build confidence, skills and meaningful connections.
